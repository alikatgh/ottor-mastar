# Changelog

## 2026-09-27 — public testing instructions

- Adds an Android tester announcement to the website news and offline news fallback.
- Adds public Google Group enrollment and separate Play opt-in instructions to the download page and README.

## 1.1.0 — submitted 2026-09-27

- Android uses the same Sardaana lily artwork as the current iOS app, with adaptive launcher sizes that keep the flower inside the safe area.
- Carries forward the botanical-only Android beta and targets Android API 36.
- Includes the current 53-plant catalog.

Version code 4 is in Google Play review. The Play Store listing also contains the matching lily artwork. The earlier local 1.0.2 candidate was superseded and was not uploaded.

## 1.0.1 — released to selected testers 2026-09-27

- Signed Android closed beta on Google Play (version code 3).
- Includes the illustrated botanical catalog, search, favorites and reading settings.
- Removes medicinal-use sections and remedy wording from Android; the beta is a botanical reference.
- Bundled catalog works offline; full-resolution images and external resources require internet.
- Closed testing uses the `ottormastar-android-testers` Google Group.

This is not an unrestricted open test or a public production release. Anyone can join the group with their Android Google account, then separately opt in on Play. Google requires at least 12 testers opted in continuously for 14 days before an application for production access.
