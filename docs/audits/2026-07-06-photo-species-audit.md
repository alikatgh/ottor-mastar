# Botanical Accuracy Audit — Field Photos vs. Declared Species

**Date:** 2026-07-06
**Scope:** 23 plant entries in the Yakutian plant guide. Each entry pairs a field photo with a declared Latin species name and a reference botanical plate. Two independent reviewers judged whether the field photo actually depicts the declared species.

---

## 1. Summary

Of the 23 entries, **8 are confirmed matches** (the field photo plausibly depicts the declared species), **15 are confirmed mismatches** (the photo's clear, in-focus subject is a different plant than the one declared), and **0 remain genuinely uncertain** after adjudication — one entry (plant-07) was flagged as a mismatch by the first reviewer but overturned to match on final review, and one entry (plant-17) was flagged by the first reviewer as uncertain but resolved to match on final review. In short: roughly two-thirds of the declared species (15 of 23) do not match what is actually pictured in the corresponding field photo, a rate high enough that it looks less like occasional field-shot clutter and more like systematic photo/caption misassignment — reinforced by several cases below where two mismatched entries appear to hold each other's photos.

## 3. Likely photo swaps

Cross-referencing the confirmed mismatches, several pairs show the classic signature of a photo-file swap: entry A's photo depicts entry B's declared species (or its family/color/form), and entry B's photo reciprocally points back toward entry A.

| Pair | Evidence | Recommended fix |
|---|---|---|
| **plant-10 ↔ plant-11** | plant-10 (declared *Filipendula ulmaria*) shows a **Vicia** vetch — exactly plant-11's declared species. plant-11 (declared *Vicia cracca*) shows a **meadowsweet/spirea-type pink-plumed plant** — exactly the form of plant-10's declared *Filipendula ulmaria*. | Swap the photo files for plant-10 and plant-11. |
| **plant-16 ↔ plant-18** | plant-18 (declared *Lathyrus pratensis*) shows **Geranium pratense** — exactly plant-16's declared species (violet-blue saucer flowers, palmate leaves). plant-16 (declared *Geranium pratense*) shows a Dianthus-type pink, which is not plant-18's declared *Lathyrus*, but the reciprocal direction (18→16's species) is a clean, high-confidence match. | Swap the photo files for plant-16 and plant-18 (verify plant-16's true subject against a *Lathyrus pratensis* reference afterward, since a residual Dianthus reading persists). |
| **plant-21 ↔ plant-22** | plant-21 (declared *Geranium sibiricum*) shows a **Valeriana/Achillea-type white-umbel plant** — exactly plant-22's declared species. plant-22 (declared *Valeriana officinalis*) shows a **wild Geranium** — exactly plant-21's declared species. | Swap the photo files for plant-21 and plant-22. |
| **plant-03 ↔ plant-04** (partial) | plant-04 (declared *Campanula glomerata*) shows **Galium verum** — exactly plant-03's declared species, and does so cleanly (frame-filling, unambiguous). plant-03 (declared *Galium verum*) shows a **Geranium**-type flower, not *Campanula glomerata*, so the reciprocal half is weaker — plant-03's photo may belong to a third, not-yet-identified entry rather than to plant-04. | Move plant-04's photo to plant-03 (high confidence). Treat plant-03's current photo (the Geranium) as unassigned/misfiled pending further review — it likely belongs to a different entry (compare against plant-16/plant-22, which also feature Geranium-type mismatches, to see if it matches one of those declared species instead). |

Entries plant-13, plant-14, plant-19, and plant-20 are also confirmed mismatches but did not pair cleanly with another mismatched entry's declared species in this dataset — their true photo sources remain unidentified and likely belong to species not included among these 23 entries (or to un-catalogued exposures).

## 5. Notes

- **plant-07 "Pratum mixtum"** is not a real botanical binomial. It is a deliberate generic entry representing a "mixed wildflower meadow" scene rather than a single identifiable species, and both the field photo and its plate caption ("Wildflower Meadow Grass") should be read in that light rather than judged against a specific taxon.
- **All reference plates in this dataset are stylised/AI-generated and their citations are fabricated** (e.g., invented "Tab." plate numbers, invented authorities/dates such as "Pallas... 1776" on plant-19, and invented engraving-style captions). This is true even where the plate's caption correctly names and correctly depicts the declared species — the accuracy of a plate's botanical rendering does not imply the citation or plate number is real. Plate captions were nonetheless useful as an independent check on what the declared species is supposed to look like, and in every reviewed case the plate itself matched its own caption (the mismatches all originate in the field photo, never in a plate misrepresenting its declared species).
