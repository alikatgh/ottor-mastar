Help test Ottor Mastar on Android 🌿

The initial Android beta is available to enrolled testers. Update 1.1.0 with the matching iOS lily logo is in Google Play review. We’re inviting testers before the public launch: an illustrated herbarium of Yakutia and Mongolia, search, favorites and reading settings. The bundled catalog works offline; full-resolution images and external resources need internet. Botanical reference, not medical advice.

Join the tester group above with the Google account you use on Android. Use the Play link above to opt in and install the available beta. The latest update appears after Google approves it. This is a closed test with group enrollment, not an unrestricted open test. If Play says the app is unavailable while review is pending, check back after approval.

Please stay opted in for at least 14 continuous days. Google requires 12 testers before we can apply for production access. Try searching, reading and offline use, then tell us what worked and what needs attention in the comments.

iPhone/iPad/Mac: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6789648576
Website and updates: https://ottormastar.aulenor.com/news
